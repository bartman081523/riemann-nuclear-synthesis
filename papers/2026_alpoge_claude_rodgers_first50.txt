Proof. Let s satisfy the three hypotheses on the left of (1). If Re s > 1/2, (5) contradicts ζ(s) = 0. If
Re s = 1/2, the conclusion already holds. It remains to exclude Re s < 1/2.
First s 6= 0, since otherwise ζ(s) = −1/2 by (i). Set w = 1 − s. Then Re w = 1 − Re s > 1/2 and
w 6= 1. For every n ∈ N, the real part of −n is nonpositive, whereas Re w > 0; hence w 6= −n. These
verify every hypothesis of (ii), so (2) applies. Its left side is zero.
The factors 2 and (2π)−w are nonzero. The factor Γ(w) is nonzero by (iii), since Re w > 0. The
factor ζ(w) is nonzero by (5), since Re w > 1/2 and w 6= 1. To handle the remaining factor, suppose
cos(πw/2) = 0. By (iv), πw/2 = (2k + 1)π/2 for an integer k. Cancelling the nonzero real number π gives
w = 2k + 1, and therefore s = −2k. If k < 0, then k ≤ −1 and Re s = −2k ≥ 2, contrary to Re s < 1/2.
